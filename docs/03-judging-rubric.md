# 03 — Judging Rubric and How We Score 5/5

Source: NASA Space Apps 2026 Judging & Awards Guide. Each criterion is scored 1–5 at every level
(Local → Global → Executive). Most of the evaluation is based on the project submission page;
the demo strongly influences Presentation.

| Criterion | What a 5 means (official, paraphrased) | Our evidence for a 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact** | Addresses a significant problem; broad reach; could inspire/benefit a large audience | Turns NASA archives into an accessible "detective" tool for students worldwide; classroom-ready case files; local (Ukraine) case; works for any region |
| **Creativity** | Novel, original approach or meaningful improvement on existing ideas | Trend map with opposite-sign regions; "years until detectable"; detective narrative; grounded SI partner |
| **Validity** | Scientifically sound, technically feasible, fully developed, usable | Deseasonalised modified MK + Sen's slope + CI; validation against known published trends; methods page; tested code; usability test with a non-expert |
| **Relevance** | Fully addresses the challenge; NASA data is core, not superficial | Every objective R-01..R-06 demonstrably done; ≥ 4 NASA sources (MERRA-2, GLDAS, GPM, MODIS/OMI/AIRS) as the engine of the tool |
| **Presentation** | Clear, engaging, well-structured storytelling | 30-s video with a hook ("Is your city really getting hotter?"), visible product, English subtitles; polished submission page with images |

## Global awards to target
- **Best Use of Science** — scientific method, honest significance testing, validation.
- **Best Use of Data** — makes NASA data accessible.
- **Best Storytelling** — detective narrative and case files.
- **Local Impact** — local case study.
- Secondary: Galactic Impact, Art & Technology.
