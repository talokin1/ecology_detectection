# ADR-005 — Case-region boxes and land-only regional means

- Status: Proposed (pending team approval; data plan decision D1)
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Deciders (names): _fill in_
- Requirement IDs: R-02, R-12, R-15

## Context
Plan v2 fixes three first-wave cases on POWER data: (1) drought in southern Ukraine (root-zone soil
wetness, precipitation), (2) warming of Ukraine (T2M), (3) opposite precipitation trends in northern and
southern Europe. Each needs a region box. Two risks:
- **Cherry-picking:** a box chosen or adjusted after seeing its trend makes the result meaningless.
- **Sea cells:** POWER precipitation has values over the sea. The northern box would be 15% sea and the
  southern 24% sea, but the questions are about land ("rain in Europe", "drought on farmland").

## Options
1. Boxes from named region definitions, whole box averaged. Simple; mixes sea into land questions.
2. Boxes from named region definitions, **land cells only**. Answers the question asked; one more processing
   step to document.
3. Boxes tuned per case by eye. Fast, but indefensible: a judge can ask "why this box?".

## Decision (proposed)
Option 2, with boxes fixed **before** any trend is computed for them:

| Preset | Box [W, S, E, N] | Basis | Land share |
|---|---|---|---|
| `south_ukraine` | [28.5, 44.4, 37.5, 48.5] | southern steppe oblasts (Odesa, Mykolaiv, Kherson, Zaporizhzhia) and Crimea | 93% |
| `ukraine` | [22.0, 44.0, 40.5, 52.5] | Q2 canonical app region: whole country including Crimea, `land_only: true` (the stats-archive box and Crimea-included polygon mask stay named alternates) | 95% |
| `north_europe` | [5.0, 55.0, 31.0, 71.0] | European part of IPCC AR6 region NEU (Iturbide et al., 2020): Fennoscandia, Denmark, Baltic states | 85% |
| `south_europe` | [-10.0, 36.0, 28.0, 45.0] | European part of IPCC AR6 region MED: Iberia, southern France, Italy, Balkans, Greece | 76% |

Land-only: a cell counts if MERRA-2 has root-zone soil wetness there (excludes ocean, lakes and ice
sheets); IMERG (0.1°) and SYN1deg (1°) cells take the nearest MERRA-2 cell's flag. Set per preset
(`land_only: true` in `data/catalog/regions.yaml`) and recorded in every series' provenance.
Alternatives considered: mainland-only southern Ukraine [28.5, 46.0, 37.5, 48.5] (99% land); NEU including
the UK and Ireland [-10, 55, 31, 71] (63% land).

## Consequences
- Boxes and the land-only rule must not change after results are seen; any change gets a new ADR.
- Sea-ice fraction cannot be used with land-only presets: it is an ocean variable (`surface: ocean` in the
  catalog) and `acquire.get_series` refuses it with "no data". Correction (2026-11-14): an earlier version of
  this ADR claimed the refusal already happened; it did not. Land cells hold 0 rather than missing, and coastal
  cells counted as land carry a little ice, so a land-only Ukraine series (84% zeros, max 0.008) had been built.
  It was removed.
- IMERG covers the whole northern box up to 71°N (checked 2026-11-14: 100% of cells in Jan and Jul), but
  satellite precipitation over snow is less reliable; MERRA-2 is the primary series, IMERG the cross-check.
- Follow-up: team approves or edits, then removes `status: proposed` from the presets.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-14, Proposed): IPCC AR6 reference regions as a second, objective region set
Context: the case boxes above were chosen by us, which limits how far the demo generalises (only Europe and
three test boxes had data) and invites the question whether regions were cherry-picked.

Decision (proposed): add the IPCC AR6 WGI land reference regions (Iturbide et al., 2020, v4) as region
presets `ar6_<acronym>` in `data/catalog/regions_ar6.yaml`, generated by `scripts/make_ar6_regions.py` from
the official vertex list vendored in `data/catalog/sources/` (CC BY 4.0, unchanged). Rule, fixed from
geometry only before any trend was computed for these regions:
- all 46 regions with Surface "Land" or "Land-Ocean" (RAR and RAR* are one region split at ±180°);
- the series are clipped to the polygon outline (cell centres inside; cells on a left/bottom edge count, cells
  on a right/top edge do not, so neighbouring regions never share a cell) and then use land cells only
  (the same land mask as above);
- not built: the ice-sheet regions GIC, EAN, WAN (the MERRA-2 land mask excludes ice sheets, so a land-only
  mean would describe only the ice-free coast) and any region with fewer than 20 MERRA-2 land cells (none);
- variables: every POWER catalog variable except sea ice (ocean variable) plus GRACE. MODIS NDVI and OMI NO2
  are not built yet: their readers take boxes only and refuse outlines (`unsupported`).

Consequences: 43 regions × 13 variables of regional series covering every inhabited continent. Results for
the case regions above can be shown next to the distribution over all AR6 regions. The `app` flag stays
false: which regions the app menu offers is a viz/team decision. The case boxes are unchanged.
