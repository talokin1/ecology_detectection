## Notable data-quality findings

1. **GSOM precipitation coverage collapses after about 2000**, and long-record TAVG has **no data in 2020-2024**. Those holes dominate any station-based trend after the 1990s.
2. **POWER all-sky solar starts in 1984**, not 1981, and official documentation places GEWEX SRB at 1984-2000 and CERES SYN1deg from 2001 (Data v10 applies quantile mapping to pre-2001 values). The on-disk header only names CERES SYN1deg and does not state a POWER data version, so this extract has **not been confirmed as Data v10**. Treating 1984-2025 as one homogeneous unadjusted series is unsafe.
3. **MODIS LST is not “warming faster than air temperature.”** The steeper LST slopes come from the shorter 2001-2025 window; POWER T2M over that same window is similar.
4. **SWOT is a 3.3-year operational record**, not a climate baseline. `ice_dyn_f` and `dschg_c` are fill in every Hydrocron row. Kherson is not a confirmed Kakhovka step (Apr-Jun difference -0.006 m, Mann-Whitney p=0.687; month FE + step HAC lag-3 p=0.530).
5. **SWOT fill-value traps:** official sentinels are `-999` and `-999999999999`. Some uncertainty fields also contain implausible values with `|x| > 1e6` (for example `width_u`). Pass 012 of the sample shapefile does not intersect Ukraine.
6. **The bbox is about twice Ukraine** and includes sea and neighbouring countries. POWER and MODIS means are bbox means, while GSOM/GHCN are Ukraine (UP) stations.
7. **GHCN-Daily precipitation in 2020-2024 rests on two stations**; 2025 temperature uses only 9 of 24 long-record stations. Partial **2026** must be dropped from climate annual trends.
8. **GES DISC extracts now on disk:** MERRA-2, GLDAS 2.0/2.1, IMERG Final, and AIRS are pulled (see `data_types_summary.md`). OMI is still downloading (partial 2004-10–2006-12 only). AppEEARS 1 km rasters are accessible but not yet pulled. GRACE is not downloaded. Hydrology Data Rods is discontinued, not blocked. KNMI and RADARSAT are still blocked.
