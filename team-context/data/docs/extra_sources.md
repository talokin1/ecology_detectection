## Ranking and independence (updated after Critique)

Priority is now driven by **new observing systems and variables**, not by how easy a 25 MB temperature grid is to cut.

- **Highest value, keep High:** ERA5 / ERA5-Land and ESA CCI soil moisture (COMBINED) add observing systems we do not already have. COMBINED is still High as a satellite retrieval, but it is **CDF-matched to GLDAS-Noah v2.1** and **break-adjusted against ERA5** (Preimesberger et al. 2021, built into COMBINED since v08.1), so it is an **anomaly/trend cross-check only**, not independent in absolute level or range, and not independent of ERA5 in the homogenization step. **CHIRPS v3** is a useful high-resolution precip field but is **not independent of GPCC or GHCN** (GTS, GHCN, and ~80k GPCC normals in CHPclim2 are inputs; missing data are filled with 0.25° ERA5).
- **Medium, same-observing-system cross-check — not confirmation:** GISTEMP 1200 km LOTI, HadCRUT5 analysis, Berkeley Earth, CRU-CY / CRU TS. They share the GHCN-D / GHCN-M / CLIMAT backbone. Annual 1981–2025 correlations among GISTEMP, HadCRUT, Berkeley, CRU-CY and POWER on this domain are ~0.976–0.993. **Keep one land grid (Berkeley 1° bbox) as the consistency check.** Do not add these cut-outs to a confirmation count in findings. No further GHCN-family grids will be downloaded.
- **GISTEMP and HadCRUT5 are land-ocean blends** (air temperature over land mixed with SST; GISTEMP also applies 1200 km smoothing). They are optional context, not core land-temperature evidence. HadCRUT5 values are official **full 5° cell means** (even a sliver cell contributes its whole-cell mean); treat HadCRUT5 as **coarse context only**. The primary GHCN-family check is the **Berkeley Earth 1° land bbox**. Cells are now selected by **overlap with the bbox**, not by whether the cell centre falls in the box (that old rule dropped all HadCRUT land north of 50°N, including Kyiv).
- **GPCC** is a useful long gauge-grid for precipitation, but it is **not independent of IMERG Final** (typically gauge-calibrated against GPCC/GPCP; 2000–2024 r≈0.981 on this bbox) or of GHCN gauges. Units are mm/month. Precipitation linear trends on this bbox: **no detectable trend; sources disagree on sign.**
- GitHub helper repos are listed only as tooling, not as data sources of the same rank.

## Notes and blockers

1. **ERA5 no-login path is ARCO Zarr; only a 1940–1951 sample was kept.** No monthly or year-chunked ARCO store was found. The hourly `ar/full_37-1h-0p25deg-chunk-1.zarr-v3` store took ~4 minutes per year, so the 1940–2026 pull was stopped after 12 complete years (method demonstration: 00/12 UTC monthly means for 2 m temperature and swvl1–4; monthly sum of hourly precipitation, m×1000 → mm; land-masked CSV). ERA5-Land is not in that ARCO bucket. A Copernicus CDS login is still needed for official CDS monthly means and for ERA5-Land / CAMS (no `CDSAPI` credentials here). Year files 1940–1951 were kept so a later run can resume.

2. **CRU TS v4.10 global grids are large** (tmp ~426 MB gzip, pre ~665 MB gzip). Public no-login OPeNDAP for v4.10 was not found: CEDA still listed through v4.09 and needs an account; IRI Data Library now requires all users to sign in. Country averages (CRU-CY) were used instead. Official CRU-CY definition files do **not** state whether Crimea is inside the Ukraine mask — that remains unverified. CRU-CY values are absolute; anomaly columns vs 1961–1990 and 1951–1980 were added so they are not overlaid on an anomaly axis raw.

3. **SPEIbase files were not retrieved.** Versioned directories timed out; a guessed `spei12.nc` path 404ed; digital.csic.es presented an Anubis proof-of-work page. **Important:** GitHub release notes for SPEIbase v2.13 (7 Nov 2026) say a PET mm/day→mm/month conversion bug affects **v2.6–v2.12**, and that the corrected dataset would be published separately on spei.csic.es. The landing page this turn still advertised v2.11 as the newest downloadable dataset and did not show a live v2.13 data tree (the `_2_13` URL timed out). Do not treat v2.6–v2.12 as analysis-ready until v2.13 (or an official all-clear) is confirmed.

4. **TerraClimate THREDDS is down from this host today** (HTTP 500 on catalog and NCSS; wget PHP unreachable). Try again later — NCSS remains the right scalable method.

5. **NOAA PSL THREDDS** (useful for GPCC and other grids) returned HTTP 429, then 502. Not used.

6. **CHIRPS v3 COG `/vsicurl/` window-reads are done** (rasterio installed this turn): 1981-01–2026-10, 550 months, bbox NetCDF + area-weighted CSV. Units are **mm/month** (the NetCDF `precip` units attribute is `mm`). **Use v3, not v2**, because v2’s 50°N cutoff omits most of Ukraine and would also fail a later global scale-up at mid-latitudes. Inputs include GTS, GHCN, and ~80,000 GPCC normals in CHPclim2, with 0.25° ERA5 fill — **not independent of GPCC or GHCN**, and not fully independent of ERA5. Precip trends: **no detectable trend; sources disagree on sign.**

7. **GRDC official data are request-only.** The open GRDC-Caravan zip is 7.6–8.8 GB (over the download cap). Ukraine’s presence in the 25-country open subset is unverified.

8. **Earthdata-gated products** (GIMMS NDVI, GRACE) need the EARTHDATA_TOKEN secret. GRACE was explicitly not downloaded here. **ESA CCI SM COMBINED v09.2 did *not* need a CEDA login** from this host: open OPeNDAP spatial subsets work (daily→monthly extract complete: 1978-11–2024-12, 554 months). Other CCI collections may still be gated — test before assuming.

9. **ClimateSERV** API root returned HTTP 200 but described ETL config endpoints, not a confirmed one-call bbox time-series API. Not relied on.

10. **Kaggle** was not used: typical datasets need an account, and ready-made Ukraine-only kernels are out of scope. **PANGAEA** was not searched to completion in this pass (no confirmed Ukraine-or-global hit recorded). Marked incomplete, not negative.

11. No national/regional-only product was treated as a primary source. Berkeley and CRU-CY “Ukraine” files are extracts of global products, but they are **country integrals**, not the analysis bbox, and the CRU-CY Ukraine mask (Crimea in or out) is unverified.

12. **GPCC gauge counts** were not retained when official chunks were discarded after the first mean was written. A re-extract should add a `gauge` column; until then recent gauge density in the box is not verifiable from the kept CSV. Units are mm/month.
