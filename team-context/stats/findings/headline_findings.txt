Methods (revised after review, 14 November 2026, Europe/Kiev)
  Trends are fit to annual or meteorological-season means for climate series (so the seasonal cycle does not dominate). OLS uses classical, AR(1)-inflated (n_eff = n(1-r)/(1+r)), and Newey–West HAC (lags 1 and 3) p-values. Theil–Sen is time-aware (scipy.stats.theilslopes vs year or decimal year). Mann–Kendall is reported as classical, Hamed–Rao with lag=3 (Hamed and Rao 1998; not the pymannkendall all-lag default), and trend-free pre-whitened (TFPW). Yue–Wang MK is stored in the table but is not used for wording (it can be anti-conservative here). The word “robust” requires HAC p < 0.05 and Hamed–Rao (lag=3) p < 0.05; classical MK p < 0.05 alone is not enough. Annual series use HAC lag-1 in that rule; monthly series use HAC lag-3. Rows with negative residual lag-1 autocorrelation are flagged as Hamed–Rao anti-conservative; when residual r is strongly negative (r < −0.3) they are not called robust on the basis of Hamed–Rao. Hamed–Rao assumes equally spaced observations, which the GSOM 2020–2024 hole and SWOT months missing after ice/dark filters violate (those rows are flagged hr_unequal_spacing). The full trend-test family has 72 tests (Kherson is excluded: it is a seasonality-confounded step check, not a trend). Bonferroni α = 0.05/72 = 0.0006944. Unadjusted HAC lag-1 p<0.05: 48; Hamed–Rao (lag=3) p<0.05: 50; both: 47; HAC Bonferroni rejects: 32; HAC Benjamini–Hochberg (FDR 0.05) rejects: 47. A shorter pre-declared primary family (11 series: annual T from POWER/GSOM/GHCN, POWER T2M 2001–2025, POWER precip, GSOM 1950–2000 precip, GHCN precip, annual NDVI, annual EVI, annual LST day and night) has Bonferroni α = 0.004545. These two family sizes (11 primary, 72 full) are also stored on each row as n_tests_in_primary_family / n_tests_in_full_family. Many tests are dependent (seasons, cities, overlapping vegetation windows, and the 1981–2025 and 2001–2025 T2M windows), so Bonferroni is conservative; BH-FDR is listed in trend_summary.csv.
  Headline that stands: 1981–2025 warming of about +0.55 to +0.67 °C/decade is robust across POWER, GSOM (with the 2020–2024 gap disclosed), and GHCN, and remains significant after AR(1)/HAC and Hamed–Rao (lag=3). SWOT is a short quality-filtered operational record, not evidence of a climate decline in river levels. Kherson is not a confirmed Kakhovka step.
