## 3. Standards checklist
Seasonality removed (anomalies) before trends • autocorrelation: HAC (lag-3 for monthly) + Hamed-Rao lag=3; flag HR as anti-conservative when residual AR < 0 • time-aware Sen slope; units per decade • min_n=8; label records under ~10 yr "too short for climate inference" • gaps and partial years (12/12 or deseasonalized) • multiple testing (Bonferroni/BH, state family size) • cross-source independence (POWER = MERRA-2; GHCN family; IMERG calibrated to GPCC; CHIRPS uses GTS/GHCN/GPCC) • no splicing of versions (GLDAS 2.0/2.1) • product breaks (POWER SW pre-2001, GLDAS-2.1 forcing, GRACE gap, OMI row anomaly, Terra/Aqua) • unit traps (IMERG mm/hr, kg m-2 s-1, Qs 3-h accumulation, CRU absolute) • spatial: cos(lat)/overlap weights, consistent land mask, coarse-grid effective cells, Crimea • start/end-point effects • wording matches the evidence (no "significant" on method-dependent results).
